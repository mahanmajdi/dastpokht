What we build

Everything a hakim was supposed to know

The classical Persian polymath moved freely between geometry, medicine, poetry and astronomy. Six departments keep that range alive here.

  1. 01

    Geometry & Mathematics

    From girih construction with compass and straightedge in Grade 4 to multivariable calculus in Grade 12.

  2. 02

    Laboratory Sciences

    Four wet labs and an electronics bench. Every senior designs, runs and defends one original experiment.

  3. 03

    Languages & Literature

    Farsi and English carried at full weight to Grade 12, with Arabic from Grade 6 and French or German by election.

  4. 04

    Design & Making

    A workshop with kilns, a laser cutter and looms. Persian tilework and CAD are taught in the same room, deliberately.

  5. 05

    Counselling

    One counsellor per 160 students, plus university guidance that starts in Grade 9 rather than the final term.

  6. 06

    Sport & Wellbeing

    Two gymnasiums, a 25m pool and compulsory daily movement — including for Grade 12, especially for Grade 12.

    Admissions · 1405–06

    Four steps, eight weeks

    We assess every applicant in person. No family is asked to commit before they have spent a morning in the building.

    Open now

    Enquiry

    A short form and a phone call. We will tell you honestly if a place is unlikely before you go further.

    Within 2 weeks

    Assessment morning

    Mathematics and language, written on campus. Grades 1–3 come for a play-based session instead.

    Within 4 weeks

    Family interview

    Student and parents, with the head of the relevant school. Forty minutes, and you ask most of the questions.

    Within 8 weeks

    Offer & enrolment

    A written offer, a fee schedule with no later additions, and a two-week window to decide.

    Come and see the vault for yourself

    Campus mornings run every Wednesday through Esfand. Bring your child — they will be given something to build.
