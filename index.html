A coach that reads your log

You say it.
It does the maths.

Type a meal the way you'd say it and every food lands with its numbers. Finish a workout and the next step is waiting: how much protein, how many carbs, how much water, and where your week stands. All of it from your own log, in your own numbers.

Amounts are optional. 2 eggs, 200 g chicken and a bowl of ghormeh sabzi all work — and anything it doesn't know, you add once and it's one tap from then on.

Refuel window

After training it counts down the two hours that matter and checks whether you've eaten.

The maths, out loud

Honest about being
an estimate.

A wrap is never exactly 640 kcal. Macro says the portion it assumed inside the food's own name — "1 large", "330 ml can", "180 g" — so you can see the guess it made and correct it in one tap instead of trusting a number with no story.

Portions stated

The assumed serving is written into the entry, not hidden behind it.

Fix it inline

Wrong amount? Tap − or + before you add it, or type the four numbers yourself.

Training

Say you're training.
It does the rest.

Tap Start workout and a timer runs on every device you're signed in on. Tap + Set between sets and a rest timer starts, tuned to how hard you're going. When you finish, rate the session and Macro tells you what to eat, how much to drink and where your week stands.

Twelve kinds of training

Weights, runs, football, swimming, combat and more — each burns energy at its own rate.
