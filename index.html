A coach that can read the log

It doesn't ask
what you ate.

Every question arrives with the day already attached — what's logged, what's left, the six days behind it. So the answers are about your food, in your numbers, not general advice about protein.

It holds two keys to the app itself: log_food adds what you mention to today, and get_day opens any earlier date. Say you ate something and it lands in the log — you never repeat yourself.

Streams as it thinks

Answers appear word by word, with a Stop button for when you've read enough.

The maths, out loud

Honest about being
an estimate.

A wrap is never exactly 640 kcal. Macro says the portion it assumed inside the food's own name — "1 large", "330 ml can", "180 g" — so you can see the guess it made and correct it in one tap instead of trusting a number with no story.

Macro estimates calories and macronutrients from text you type. Estimates can be wrong, sometimes by a lot, and nothing here is medical or dietary advice — for anything involving a condition, a medication or a symptom, talk to a professional.
